# Brand system

Every image is an editorial still life communicating one visual metaphor. Keep these constants across the gallery:

- Warm paper-toned environment, deep ink shadows, and one vivid accent.
- Calm directional studio light, precise contemporary materials, and tactile detail.
- One strong focal object, generous negative space, and a readable 4:5 silhouette.
- Refined glass, paper, metal, ceramic, or other materials chosen to support the claim.

Vary the focal object, supporting props, material mix, and accent hue with the topic. Props explain the relationship in the story; decoration must not compete with the focal object. Consistency comes from the palette structure, lighting, composition, and material quality, rather than repeating the same objects.

## Generation prompt contract

Use this base with a concrete, topic-specific metaphor inserted after the first sentence:

```text
Create a 4:5 editorial still-life illustration at 1600×2000. Communicate one visual metaphor.
Warm paper-toned environment, deep ink shadows, one vivid accent, precise contemporary materials,
calm studio lighting, generous negative space, tactile detail. Adapt objects and accent hue to the topic.
No text, letters, numbers, UI labels, logos, watermarks, trademarks, signatures, captions, or poster typography.
```

For example, a bidirectional design conversation can become a physical bridge between translucent layout frames and abstract mechanical structures, with material signals travelling both ways. Use an electric violet accent; keep every frame and mechanism free of readable symbols.

## Visual acceptance

The deliverable is a pure visual PNG at exactly **1600×2000**. Verify the file itself, then inspect the image: one legible metaphor, a clear focal object, warm paper/deep ink/one accent, refined materials, and no embedded words or marks. Titles, explanations, source attribution, and captions belong in the separate Markdown. If generated text or a logo appears, correct the image through the installed image-generation workflow and inspect again; small or correctly spelled text still fails this contract.
